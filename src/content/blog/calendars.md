<h3>The current season is "Chom" (חֹם — heat, H2527) Second half of Av + Elul + the first half of Tishrei</h3>

<mark>Current season is Chom (Heat)</mark>, and indeed the whole watch-community has felt the heat of the world and the watch! It is the <b>last of the summer seasons</b>, ie. <b>seasons of the end</b>.
<br><br>
This season focuses on completing the summer harvest and beginning the transition to the rainy season:Intense heat; streams and vegetation dry up (especially Av).
Vintage and processing of grapes (wine-making).
Second harvest and drying of figs and other summer fruits (pomegranates, dates).
Olive harvest and oil production (especially toward Tishrei).
Toward mid-Tishrei: early (first) rains begin; plowing and sowing of winter grains start.
These tasks reflect the “end of the hot season” before the agricultural year resets with the rains.
<br><br>
The same season form a major spiritual turning point in the Jewish year — moving from mourning to intense repentance and renewal Mid-to-late Av. After Tisha B’Av (9 Av, national mourning for the Temples), the mood shifts. Tu B’Av (15 Av) is a joyful day historically associated with matchmaking, dancing, and new beginnings.
<br><br>
Elul: The classic month of teshuvah (repentance and return). Daily shofar blowing after morning prayers, recitation of Selichot (penitential prayers), increased Torah study, charity, and self-examination. Chassidic teaching describes this time as “the King is in the field” — God is especially accessible.
<br><br><mark>
Early Tishrei (first half)</mark>: Rosh Hashanah (1–2 Tishrei) — Day of Judgment, shofar blasts, and the start of the Ten Days of Repentance. This leads directly into Yom Kippur. The season of Chom thus encompasses the climax of the High Holiday period of judgment, atonement, and spiritual reset.
