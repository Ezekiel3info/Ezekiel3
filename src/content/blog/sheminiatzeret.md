Shemini Atzeret is the 8th day after the 7-day Feast of Tabernacles. It concludes the Year of Biiblical Feasts of the LORD and three (3) days after it the Reading of the Torah also resets back to Bereshit, the beginning in Genesis.

In the Menorah -idiom, the Feast of Tabernacles sits in the candle on the opposite side of Unleavened Bread and <mark>Shemini Atzeret</mark> - <b>The Day after</b> Tabernacles, fits into the pattern of <mark>Passover</mark> being <b>The Day before</b> Unleavened Bread.

<h4>Celebration of the Dedication of the Altar</h4>

2 Chronicles 7:8-10</br>
'Also at the same time Solomon kept the feast seven days, and all Israel with him, a very great congregation, from the entering in of Hamath unto the river of Egypt. And in the <mark>eighth day they made a solemn assembly</mark>: for they kept the <b>dedication of the altar seven days</b>, and the feast seven days. And on the <mark>three and twentieth day of the seventh month</mark> <b>he sent the people away into their tents</b>, glad and merry in heart for the goodness that the LORD had shewed unto David, and to Solomon, and to Israel his people. '

> The Feast for seven (7) days is the Feast of Tabernacles, so the Shemini Atzeret, the 8th day, falls on <mark>23rd of Tishrei</mark>.</br>
> Counting back from the beginning of Feast of Tabernacles, the Altar in the 1st Temple was dedicated for seven (7) days, hence that dedication ended on 14th and begun on 7th of Tishrei.</br>
> When Yeshua, Jesus, was on the Cross in Nissan 14th, that ended the <b>dedication</b> of the <mark>Altar of the 3rd Temple</mark>
