Sukkot (the Feast of Tabernacles / Booths) is the seven-day appointed time at the end of the harvest, with an eighth day after it. Israel was told to live in temporary shelters and to rejoice before the Lord.

## When it falls

Sukkot begins on the 15th day of the seventh month on the biblical calendar (Tishrei). The dates on the Gregorian calendar move each year.

## What the Torah commands

- Live in a sukkah (booth) for seven days.
- Rejoice and gather.
- Bring the harvest festival offerings as given in the Law.
- The seventh month also holds the Feast of Trumpets and the Day of Atonement before Sukkot.

See Leviticus 23:33–43, Deuteronomy 16:13–15, and Numbers 29:12–38.
