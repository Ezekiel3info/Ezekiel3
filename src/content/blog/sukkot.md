## When is Sukkot observed

Sukkot begins on the 15th day of the seventh month on the biblical calendar, so 15th of 7th month Tishrei. The dates on the Gregorian calendar move each year, but in 2026 it is observed from 25th September until 2nd October with 3rd October being Shemini Atzeret. So <b>2026-09-25 - 2026-10-03</b>.
